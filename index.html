Privacy Policy — Playtext Teleprompter

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Publisher and scope

App: Playtext Teleprompter 6.0 (Android package com.leo.teleprompter).
Publisher: NORTHMAN (ЧП «NORTHMAN»)
Privacy contact: greenday_16@bk.ru
This policy covers the Android app. Reading this policy does not itself grant advertising consent or permission to use your camera or microphone.

2. Camera, microphone and recordings

With Android permissions, the camera supplies the preview and video recording; the microphone supplies sound for recordings and, separately, optional dictation. CameraX processes camera frames on the device. Recordings are saved as MP4 files in Android shared media storage, normally Movies/Teleprompter, and are listed in the app and compatible gallery apps. The app does not implement an upload of these recordings or camera frames to a developer server. Media files can contain your voice, appearance and surroundings. They are not private app files accessible only to Playtext.

3. Scripts, drafts and preferences

Script titles, text, identifiers and creation/update times are stored in the app's private SQLite database. Drafts, reading position, interface language, style settings and recovery state are stored locally in app preferences, WebView storage and recovery files. Import reads the TXT file you select through Android's file picker. Playtext does not implement developer-server synchronization or upload of script text. Software keyboards, accessibility tools and other services you enable may handle text under their own settings and policies.

4. Optional dictation

Dictation uses the speech-recognition service selected by Android. That service receives microphone audio and returns recognized text, which can become part of your script. Recognition may send audio to the service provider's servers; the app does not force on-device-only recognition. The provider, processing location and retention depend on the service and its settings. Playtext does not save a separate dictation audio file or send it to its own speech server. You can type instead and cancel dictation.

5. Google AdMob

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads. Google and advertising partners may receive IP-derived general location, advertising/app-set or applicable account identifiers, ad/app interactions and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement and abuse prevention. Playtext does not supply script text or recordings to the SDK as advertising content. The app does not request GPS location permission. Debug builds use Google demo advertising IDs; release builds use publisher IDs. Test ads do not imply that SDK network processing is absent.

6. Consent and privacy choices

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests consent information and displays privacy messages where required by the configured region and audience. Consent choices/status are kept on the device and used by the advertising integration. Fetching messages involves requests to Google. Advertising requests are gated by UMP's canRequestAds status and the app's FREE/PRO state. Where UMP requires a privacy-options form, use Advertising settings in a library to review your choices. Declining personalized advertising is not necessarily a ban on all advertising; availability depends on UMP and the applicable configuration. Android camera/microphone permissions are separate from advertising consent.

7. Google Play Billing and PRO

Google Play Billing handles the one-time product tp_premium_lifetime; there are no subscriptions in the app. New purchases are disabled in this test version. The app can query existing purchases for restoration and process product IDs, purchase tokens, signed purchase details and acknowledgement/state. Verified acknowledged purchase details are cached in private preferences to recognize PRO. A verified PRO entitlement suppresses banner advertising. Payment-card details are handled by Google Play, not entered into or accessed by Playtext. The app has no separate user account or developer payment server. Google controls its transaction records and associated retention.

8. Network access, diagnostics and other recipients

Internet access supports the Google advertising, consent and billing services; speech recognition can also use the network through its provider. The app's teleprompter interface is bundled locally in a restricted WebView, not loaded from a developer website. Playtext does not implement a separate developer analytics or crash-upload service. Technical errors can be written to Android system logs; this does not eliminate diagnostics handled by the advertising SDK or Android/Google Play services. If you choose Share/Open in another app, Android grants the chosen app temporary read access to the selected video. That recipient's handling is outside Playtext's control.

9. Android and media backups

Android app backup is enabled in this version without custom exclusions. Depending on Android, the device manufacturer, your account and backup settings, private app data may be included in cloud backup or device transfer. Gallery/media backup services you enable may separately copy videos. Playtext does not configure a developer cloud store for your scripts or videos, but this is not a promise that no copy can leave the device through system or other services.

10. Retention and deletion

Scripts and preferences remain in local storage until changed or removed. You can delete scripts in their library and recordings in the video library or Android gallery. Recovery journals, migration copies, database remnants or backups can retain earlier content; ordinary deletion is not a secure-erasure guarantee. Android's Clear storage or uninstall removes private app data according to Android's behavior, but shared videos and copies already shared, exported or backed up may remain. The app does not enforce fixed retention periods on Google, speech providers, recipients or backup services. Manage those copies through the relevant service or contact its provider. Clearing app data does not erase Google Play purchase history.

11. Permissions and controls

Camera and microphone access require Android permissions and can be revoked in Android settings; this can prevent the related feature from working. On Android 9 and earlier the app can request shared-storage write permission for recording. SDKs also declare network, billing, advertising-ID and service-related permissions. Playtext does not request precise/approximate device-location, contacts or SMS permissions. Advertising identifiers can be managed in Android's advertising privacy settings, subject to the Android version. All existing functions and themes are free; banners are limited to script/video libraries, not camera, reading, recording or the video player.

12. Security, contact and changes

Android's application sandbox protects private app storage; shared media has different access rules. The app disallows cleartext HTTP in its manifest, but this is not a guarantee about every external provider or service. No security mechanism guarantees absolute protection. For questions or requests relating to this policy, use the privacy contact in section 1. Requests concerning data held by Google or another provider may also need to be directed to that provider. The policy must be updated when the app's data practices change.
